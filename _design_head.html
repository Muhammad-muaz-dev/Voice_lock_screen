Voice Lock: full app flow

Drag to move around. Pinch or Ctrl + scroll to zoom.
1

First time opening the app

The user picks a language, flips through three intro pages and then sees the Pro offer. They can close it and land on Home, or buy Pro.

Comes from: Opening the app for the first time
Goes to: Home (flow 2)

2

Home screen

This is where people turn the voice commands on or off. The first time they switch one on, we ask about notifications. Pressing back asks if they really want to leave.

Comes from: Onboarding, or any time the app is opened
Goes to: Voice setup (flow 3) the first time a toggle is turned on. Camera tile: flow 7. Pin, Pattern and Fingerprint tiles: flow 5. Themes and Voice Note tabs: flows 8 and 9. Gear icon: Settings (flow 10). Crown icon: Go Pro (flow 1).

3

Setting up the voice password

Read the guide, say a phrase, allow the microphone, pick a language if needed, and check that we heard it right.

Comes from: Turning on a voice command for the first time
Goes to: Security question (flow 4)

4

Security question and permissions

Pick a question to recover the password, type the answer, then allow the permissions the app needs.

Comes from: The voice password is done
Goes to: The success screen, then flow 5

5

Extra ways to unlock

After setup the app suggests a pattern, a PIN and a fingerprint as backups, then asks for a rating.

Comes from: The success screen
Goes to: Home (flow 2)

6

The lock screen

What people see when the phone is locked. They can speak to unlock, or switch to PIN, pattern or fingerprint.

Comes from: The phone gets locked
Goes to: The phone is unlocked

7

Open Camera by voice

Shows the commands, asks for permissions, records the camera phrase, then lets people control the camera with their voice.

Comes from: Home, Open Camera tile
Goes to: Back to Home

8

Themes tab

Make a quote with AI, or upload a picture to use on the lock screen.

Comes from: The Themes tab
Goes to: The lock screen (flow 6)

9

Voice Note tab

Record a note and get the text plus a short AI summary. When the free tries run out, people can watch an ad or go Pro.

Comes from: The Voice Note tab
Goes to: Go Pro (flow 1)

10

Settings

Language, security question, system lock, share, feedback, privacy and rating.

Comes from: Home, gear icon
Goes to: Language (flow 3), security question (flow 4), rating (flow 5), Go Pro (flow 1)
